# Painting with Python

These pages use the same engine for pictures that are not maps. They cover curves painted with the brush and drawn with the pen, one motif repeated by mirror and rotation, fractals, layered hills with a lettered name, a small vector drawing washed and inked, and one stroke cased, lit and shadowed. You need only the first tutorial, [Paint a watercolour](../paint-a-watercolour.md).

- [Curves: a rose and a spirograph in brush, a Lissajous figure in pen](curves.md)
- [Symmetry: one motif washed and inked, then repeated by mirror and rotation](symmetry.md)
- [Fractals: a Barnsley fern, a family of ferns and a recursive tree](fractals.md)
- [Pictures: layered hill washes and a lettered Derwentwater](pictures.md)
- [Vector drawing: a small drawing, its filled shapes washed and its strokes inked](vector-drawing.md)
- [Cased strokes: case, light and shadow one stroke, and a pen casing](cased-strokes.md)
